# Session Summary — 2026-06-04

## Concise Recap

The session began in **Krezk** at the blessed pool, where the party had been directed by the burgomaster. The pool restores madness when touched and shows each visitor an idealized version of themself in its reflection. After lingering at the pool, the party regrouped to pursue their immediate objective at the **Abbey of Saint Markovia**: locate the book about the **Fanes** that Rudolph van Richten requested. They also received an urgent local request from Krezk's burgomaster. During the previous day's harvest, werewolves attacked outside the town walls in broad daylight, killed a farmer, killed the burgomaster's daughter Alana, and abducted two ten-year-old boys, including his son Ilya. The burgomaster asked the party to find the children. He said the attackers fled east toward the hills and lake, where Krezk had formerly maintained a grim arrangement: villagers left livestock for the werewolves at the full moon, and the pack did not attack people.

The burgomaster added another troubling detail: someone has recently been digging up the graves of Krezk's dead, including the newly buried Alana. He has assigned a farmer to watch the graveyard. The party clarified that werewolves can have humanoid, wolf, and hybrid forms, so the burgomaster's description of bipedal wolf-creatures identified the attackers as werewolves rather than ordinary wolves. This establishes two linked Krezk leads: recover Ilya and the other boy from the werewolves, and investigate the grave robber or grave robbers.

The party climbed the steep, mist-covered hill to the Abbey. Aniela found tracks leading toward the grounds that did not appear human; the party interpreted them as something like a one-legged bear following a one-legged human. At the outer gate, **Ah'man Poo'tit'en** rushed ahead, startled two sleeping **mongrelfolk** guards, and slipped into the Abbey's courtyard. The guards were named Otto and Zigfried (name transcription uncertain). Ah'man hid in a tree and shot Zigfried with a shortbow, killing him with a Sneak Attack. Diesel then broke the outer gate's latch, entered with a silvered heavy crossbow, and intimidated Otto into surrendering. Frangela prevented an immediate execution long enough to ask for help finding the library.

Otto described the Abbey as housing dozens of his "kin" and said they did not read; he referred the party to the Abbot and mentioned someone who had been "perfected" and sent away. He described the Abbey as a refuge against an outside world that rejects mongrelfolk. The party did not use him as a guide. The transcript contains a declared missing section shortly after this exchange, so any events within that gap are unavailable; the subsequent exploration nevertheless makes clear that the party entered the inner grounds and split into multiple groups.

The Abbey's inner courtyard held a well, sheds, buildings, and apparent guards. Aniela recognized that the figures on the walls and near the gate were only scarecrows dressed in improvised guard clothing, not living sentries. The group damaged or threw down several of these false guards. Ah'man searched locked sheds and killed two isolated mongrelfolk without meaningful interaction: one feathered, stork-featured creature and one man with duck feet. He later shot **Mishka**, a winged, bat-featured mongrelwoman chained to the well, after she bit Jobadia and Aniela and called for someone named Cyrus. The party recognized Cyrus as the mongrelfolk they had previously encountered at Castle Ravenloft.

Diesel investigated the long, barred outer wing while Frangela searched for the library. Through the windows, they saw disturbed, frightened, and apparently institutionalized mongrelfolk. One room contained several agitated residents; one clutched a polished brass candlestick as if it were a doll. Another group was chanting meaningless pseudo-magic in an attempt to summon dinner. Rather than wait for information or a peaceful route, Diesel began attacking the occupants. He failed to create the intended explosive chemical attack but broke a window, spilled basic poison and oil, and threw in a torch, starting a fire and filling the room with smoke. He then forced his way through loosened bars, entered the room enraged with his executioner's greataxe, and killed the remaining mongrelfolk in rapid succession. These killings, as well as Ah'man's, were directed at Abbey residents who were confined, frightened, or otherwise non-hostile at the moment of attack.

Meanwhile, **Jobadia Baskin** took dire-wolf form and raced through the Abbey. He met a two-headed mongrelfolk musician with reptilian, goat, lobster, bear, and human traits. The creature introduced itself only as a fiddler and was frightened of the party. Jobadia warned it to hide because his companions were hostile to mongrelfolk; the musician ran away. In the belfry, Jobadia found a shrouded table that initially appeared to hold a vivisected Nazareth. He resisted the resulting horror and recognized it as an illusion. Under the shroud were preserved female body parts, apparently retained for later use. This, combined with the stitched woman encountered elsewhere, strongly suggested that the Abbey's inhabitants are assembling or maintaining a constructed woman.

**Aniela Grinfeather** found the Abbey's principal residents near a stairway: the **Abbot**, an extraordinarily handsome man in Morninglord robes and holy symbol, and **Vasilka**, a woman in an old bridal gown whose face and hands visibly consisted of stitched pieces of different women. The Abbot reacted to the party's loose dire wolf with alarm but did not immediately attack. When Aniela asked about the library, he confirmed that the Abbey's books are old and precious, were placed there by Saint Markovia, and are upstairs. He repeatedly insisted that the wolf must not urinate on them and directed the party toward the graveyard latrine instead. Vasilka spoke simply and matter-of-factly, mentioning that **Cloven** often urinates upstairs and that the Abbot sometimes locks him in a shed.

The party's exploration revealed that the Abbey is not a normal monastery or library but a deteriorated asylum/workshop guarded by mongrelfolk and scarecrows. Its rooms include barred bedchambers, locked sheds, old barracks, a belfry, an inner graveyard, and a body-part storage area. The Abbot's relationship to the mongrelfolk and Vasilka remained unclear, but the site contains obvious evidence of confinement, bodily modification, and medical or magical experimentation. The party had not yet found the Fanes book by the end of the available transcript.

The session ended with Jobadia, still in dire-wolf form, encountering the Abbot at a locked reinforced door. The Abbot correctly recognized him as a druid rather than an ordinary wolf, greeted him as "friend druid," and offered to open the door only if Jobadia would help him with something. The nature of the Abbot's requested favor was not revealed before the session ended.
