# Session Summary — 2026-07-30

## Concise Recap

The party's assault on **Councillor Dory's warehouse** escalated into a catastrophic combat that resulted in the building's destruction. As the party infiltrated the warehouse and climbed the interior stairwells toward Dory's crane-suspended ship, they encountered **manticores** (flying creatures serving as guards) and **Perin** opened fire with alchemical weapons, causing massive structural damage. The conflict degenerated into a series of desperate survival situations with multiple party members falling unconscious and taking death saving throws. The building collapsed around them, killing the scum still present and forcing the party to fight their way out while managing severely wounded or dying allies.

Amid the chaos, **Aniela Grinfeather** was severely wounded and left on the crane platform at the ship's entrance, stabilized by **Perin** using the *spare the dying* cantrip but unable to receive healing. The party managed to escape the burning warehouse as villagers arrived with buckets to fight the spreading fire. The group regrouped outside, recovering for approximately two hours before dawn while the fire was gradually extinguished by the community.

Despite the destruction, the party discovered that **Perin** could access his warlock patron's gifts even in this foreign land, granting him a 10-minute window of flight. Using this ability, Perin disguised himself as a **skum** and infiltrated the still-standing crane and its suspended ship structure to gather intelligence and locate **Mr. Dory**. He discovered that Dory had retreated into the ship's interior after witnessing the warehouse's destruction and was extremely angry about the catastrophe. The party also learned that several **skum** remained alive on the crane structure and in the ship, serving as guards and maintaining Dory's operations.

The party then executed a coordinated rescue and infiltration operation. Using ropes anchored to cleats on the ship, the party members crossed a rickety wooden bridge suspended 60 feet above the mud pit. **Aniela**, barely alive and weakened, was hauled across the dangerous bridge with support from her allies. Once aboard the ship, the party encountered more **skum** guards attempting to maintain order and protect Dory's interests. A combat erupted involving the party members, multiple skum with tridents, and eventually a **hippopotamus** (Reggae Joe's wild shape form), which dramatically displaced water and pushed enemies around the ship's hot-water compartment.

The session concluded with the party engaging in brutal combat against the skum in the ship's boiler room and flooded compartment, with party members taking heavy damage and nearly drowning in superheated, poisoned water. **Aniela** received healing before entering the building, restoring her to better condition. Perin explored the ship's interior, discovering evidence of Dory's presence and additional guard positions. The party remained focused on confronting Dory directly, but the challenges of navigating the ship's structure, managing the flooded compartments, and fighting multiple enemies suggested that the final confrontation would be difficult and dangerous.
