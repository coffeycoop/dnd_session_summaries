# Session Summary — 2026-06-11

## Concise Recap

The party continued its chaotic exploration of the **Abbey of Saint Markovia** in Krezk while still seeking the book about the **Fanes** for Rudolph van Richten. At the start of the session, the players recapped that the Abbey is effectively an asylum and workshop occupied by magically created **mongrelfolk**, and that the party had already killed several residents and guards. Nazareth consulted *Volo's Guide* and confirmed that mongrelfolk are not naturally occurring creatures: they are magical human-animal hybrids. This strengthened the party's suspicion that the Abbot, who was assembling **Vasilka** from body parts, may also have created the Abbey's mongrelfolk.

On the upper battlements, **Jobadia Baskin**—still in animal form—spoke with the Abbey's leader, who introduced himself as **the Abbot**. The Abbot described the Abbey as a hospital for the sick and himself as its healer. He recognized the party's interest in the library and immediately connected them to the earlier visit of Dr. van Richten, whom he described as rude and contrary. He refused to freely allow access to the books in his private office, saying that no one had previously wanted them until van Richten and the party arrived. The party's request for the Fanes book therefore became a direct negotiation with the Abbot rather than a simple library search.

The Abbot's requested price was explicit and morally unacceptable to the party. He said Vasilka needed one final piece to be complete: the face of **Alana Krezkov**, the freshly buried daughter of Krezk's burgomaster, who had been killed in the recent daylight werewolf attack. The Abbot admitted that he had been sending **Clovin**, a mongrelfolk with an elephant foot, into Krezk's graveyard to take the faces of recently deceased young women. The town had discovered the grave robbing and posted guards, preventing Clovin from retrieving Alana's body. The Abbot offered to let the party read the desired book only if they procured Alana's face.

Nazareth confronted the Abbot over his allegiance to Strahd and his corpse theft. The Abbot denied serving Strahd, stated that he serves the Morninglord, and claimed his plan was to build Strahd a perfect bride so that Strahd would stop preying upon Barovian women. He argued that only he was willing to make the hard choices needed to protect Krezk. Nazareth rejected that justification, prepared to fight, and opened hostilities with a critical hit. Jobadia also antagonized the Abbot by repeatedly kicking him while demanding the book. Combat began on the upper battlements while other party members were elsewhere in the Abbey.

At the same time, Frangela spoke with Vasilka. Vasilka was naive, sincere, and childlike in her understanding of the world; she had never been allowed outside. She was making a garland as a gift for the unnamed flesh golem that guards the asylum, because she believed love meant giving presents. She wanted to go to Castle Ravenloft to be with her intended groom, apparently Strahd, but was forbidden from approaching the golem because the Abbot believed it might hurt her. Frangela promised to help her see the golem, then instead tried to protect and remove her from the Abbey. Frangela used *message* to warn Aniela that Vasilka was a victim, took her outside, distracted her from the bodies and violence, and began disguising her with a wig, mustache, monk's cloak, and later a leather patch over the missing area of her face. Vasilka was delighted by the patch and said she was now ready to go to her groom. Frangela intended to bring her to Krezk's burgomaster before allowing that.

On the lower level, Ah'man pursued a fleeing mongrelfolk carrying a spoon and encountered the Abbey's **flesh golem** in the hall. The golem was a large, badly proportioned stitched construct, apparently the being Vasilka wanted to give her garland to. Ah'man hid around a corner, shot it with Sneak Attack, and used his Sneak Attack Trip feature, though the golem resisted the attempt to knock it prone. Diesel rushed into the hall enraged, toppled the golem with his greataxe, struck it repeatedly, and killed additional mongrelfolk who tried to flee. He later used Bardic Inspiration on Ah'man. Ah'man climbed onto the golem's back, stabbed it repeatedly until it went berserk, then disengaged, returned to Diesel, and destroyed it with a shortbow shot that tore apart its central stitching. The lower asylum wing was effectively cleared, though at least one frightened mongrelfolk escaped into another room or toward the grounds.

The confrontation with the Abbot revealed that he is not merely a human monk. He conjured a radiant spiritual mace, used blinding light and psychic magic, and after taking damage revealed feathered wings. His eyes glowed with a disturbing swirl of white, pink, and black. During the fight, he repeatedly sent **Nazareth**, **Jobadia**, and **Aniela** into psychic visions of his past. These visions forced each target to make moral choices analogous to choices the Abbot had made, with psychic damage and madness as consequences.

Nazareth's first vision placed him in the Abbot's body while two Barovian men asked to be healed after being cursed for stealing food during a famine. The men said they had stolen only to feed their children and had come to the Abbey for healing. Nazareth refused to participate in the vision, suffered psychic damage, and became catatonic for a time. A later vision showed wealthy members of the **Belview** family demanding animal traits as "healing": eagle eyes, mountain-lion teeth, and goat legs. Nazareth again rejected the demand. The visions strongly imply that the Abbot once transformed supplicants into the mongrelfolk now confined in the Abbey, even if the exact historical choices remain inferred rather than directly witnessed.

Jobadia's vision showed the Abbey in an earlier, functional state, treating wounded members of the **Order of the Silver Dragon** alongside Barovian patients. **Rahadin** (name inferred from the transcript's phonetic rendering and Strahd context) arrived and threatened to destroy the Abbey and kill its Barovian patients if the Abbot continued sheltering the Order. Jobadia chose to expel the knights, matching the Abbot's apparent historical decision. He suffered psychic damage and long-term madness: for six days, he believes he is a werewolf and the leader of all Barovian werewolves. Aniela successfully resisted the Abbot's psychic assault and later struck Nazareth to help end his earlier madness-induced stupor, though she could not end the active psychic spell immediately.

Nazareth used *Nature's Veil* to turn invisible, moved out of his known position, and attacked the Abbot with arrows. The Abbot answered with a **wall of fire** placed where he expected Nazareth to be, then attacked Jobadia's summoned fire elemental. Jobadia dismissed his dire-wolf form, summoned a fire elemental using a 6th-level spell, and later wild-shaped into a goat while continuing to demand the book. The elemental damaged the Abbot, but the party did not defeat him during the available session.

The session ended with the group split across the Abbey. Frangela was escorting Vasilka and Pidlwick toward the exit and planned to involve Krezk's burgomaster. Aniela had moved downstairs to find and assist Nazareth and Jobadia. The Abbot remained alive and in combat, now visibly winged and wielding powerful divine and psychic magic. The Fanes book had not been recovered, Vasilka's future was unresolved, and Jobadia was suffering an active six-day delusion of werewolf leadership.

## Entities

- **The Abbot:** Abbey leader and healer; secretly a winged, powerful celestial-like being. He makes and alters people, seeks to complete Vasilka as a bride for Strahd, and uses psychic visions to test or punish opponents.
- **Vasilka:** Stitched woman being prepared as Strahd's bride; has a naive, childlike understanding of love and the outside world; wants to go to Castle Ravenloft; is being escorted away by Frangela.
- **Clovin:** Mongrelfolk servant with an elephant foot; the Abbot sends him to steal body parts from Krezk's graveyard.
- **Flesh golem:** Large stitched construct that guarded the asylum wing; wanted by Vasilka as the recipient of her garland; destroyed by Ah'man and Diesel.
- **Belview family:** Aristocratic family seen in Nazareth's vision, demanding animal traits from the Abbot as forms of "healing." Their requests are evidence about the mongrelfolk's origin.
- **Rahadin (inferred):** Appeared in Jobadia's historical vision as Strahd's gray-skinned elf representative, threatening the Abbey over its support for the Order of the Silver Dragon.
- **Pidlwick:** Accompanied Frangela and Vasilka near the Abbey exit; reacted with curiosity to Vasilka's incomplete face.

- The Abbot is far more than a normal cleric or monk: he has wings, a spiritual mace, radiant attacks, psychic magic, and *wall of fire*.
