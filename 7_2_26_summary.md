# Session Summary — 2026-07-02

## Concise Recap

The party continued the battle at the **werewolf den** against **Kiril Stoyanovich**, the Strahd-aligned usurper of the pack. The party had already recovered the kidnapped children, with **Sildar Hallwinter** helping take them out of the den. Jobadia Baskin / Reggae Joe was still under his six-day madness delusion that he was the king of Barovia's werewolves. In an enormous wolf form, he attempted to assert authority over the den rather than immediately kill its inhabitants. Kiril transformed into a nine-foot-tall hybrid werewolf and attacked Jobadia with claws and a bite, inflicting severe damage and attempting to transmit lycanthropy. Jobadia resisted the infection.

The fight also revealed that Kiril had brought dire wolves back from Castle Ravenloft as part of Strahd's blessing. The dire wolves did not recognize Jobadia's claim to be the new alpha; one appeared driven by a separate, hostile influence and kept fighting after Kiril's defeat. Ah'man was injured by falling debris from the ongoing earth-shaking effect and fell unconscious, while Pidlwick was badly damaged in the fight. Diesel used *healing word* to restore Ah'man to consciousness, and the party continued fighting around the dangerous, disrupted terrain.

Jobadia ultimately defeated Kiril in close combat while still presenting himself as the pack's alpha. Kiril reverted to human form, badly wounded, and said that he feared Strahd's wrath more than Jobadia's. **Davanka**, a pack member who had been waiting to see who would win, gave Kiril a thumbs-down and encouraged his execution. Jobadia killed him by crushing his neck. His death ended the earth-shaking effect, though the battlefield remained strewn with rubble and difficult terrain.

The remaining hostile dire wolf then attacked Jobadia and forced him out of his giant wolf form. Diesel responded by summoning a spectral pack of wolves with *conjure animals*, framing them as support for the presumed alpha. Frangela used *chill touch* against Kiril before his death, invoking Mother Night and asking the pack to recognize its rightful leadership; her magic stopped Kiril from healing and helped sway Davanka. A rogue party member then killed the final dire wolf with a shot between the eyes. The immediate combat ended with Kiril and the hostile dire wolf dead, the children safe, and the surviving werewolves no longer in open conflict with the party.

The party then negotiated with the surviving pack. **Zuleika** refused Jobadia's confused proposal to become his mate and stated that her husband, **Emil**, remains her chosen partner. She offered the pack's loyalty against Strahd if the party rescues Emil. Zuleika explained that Kiril had returned from Ravenloft with dire wolves and Strahd's favor, declared that Strahd replaced Mother Night, and had Emil bound and taken to **Tsolenka Pass** on the road toward the Amber Temple. There, one of Strahd's consorts is said to hold him in a fortified place and experiment on people. The party accepted this as an active future objective, though its immediate plan was to return to Krezk.

Loot and aftermath in the den included a **potion of greater healing** and a commemorative gold coin bearing Strahd's face, apparently a token identifying Kiril's allegiance rather than normal currency. Ah'man also pickpocketed **Skennis**, an elderly pack member, and acquired **dust of disappearance**. The dust can make the user, nearby creatures, and nearby objects invisible for a short duration; it is a one-use item. The party healed Ah'man and discussed repairing Pidlwick. A party member began working on an upgrade that would eventually allow Pidlwick to make a distinctive bird-call alert and potentially deploy oil, but this work requires downtime and was not complete during the session.

The party returned to **Krezk**. They learned that Ilya Krezkov and the two other kidnapped boys had returned safely, confirming the rescue's success. The burgomaster fed the group and gave them the promised magical family heirloom: an **In-Spelled Scroll**. The scroll can permanently apply one specified spell to a weapon or armor item for one daily use, with attunement required. The available choices were *feather fall*, *magic missile*, *alter self*, *enhance ability*, *find steed*, and *misty step*. The party chose *misty step* for the item, though the specific item and eventual attuned wearer were not finalized in the transcript.

The group accepted another long rest in Krezk. During the rest, work continued on Pidlwick's planned alert feature. In the morning, the party purchased three flasks of oil and used Krezk's blessed pool where possible. The pool removed the party's recent temporary madness effects for those who had not already used it; it could not cure Jobadia's long-term delusion because he had already benefited from the pool earlier.

The party then returned to **Van Richten's Tower** to report the werewolf outcome. Van Richten accepted that the immediate problem had been resolved and revealed the last major information he had withheld about the **Amber Temple** and **Vampyr**. He said the Amber Temple was built as a prison for dark gods from Barovia's original world. Vampyr's amber prison cracked, allowing Vampyr to beckon Strahd after Strahd committed terrible crimes against his family. Vampyr made Strahd immortal and feeds on Strahd's victims. Therefore, even if the party weakens and defeats Strahd by reconsecrating the Fanes, Strahd will regenerate unless they reseal Vampyr's essence in its amber prison.

Van Richten described the materials for the resealing ritual: salt, six blessed candles, a cubic foot of amber, and a lock of hair from a living evil person. The Amber Temple itself should contain enough amber, and the party expects to acquire the ordinary supplies before traveling. He also warned that the Temple is extremely cold in the current season. Van Richten urged the party to recover the **Sunsword** there as well. It once belonged to Strahd's brother **Sergei** and was broken by Strahd after Sergei's death. The sword may need powerful magical repair; van Richten believes the missing archmage **Mordenkainen** could be capable of restoring it.

This led to renewed discussion of the Mad Mage. The party recalled a riddle about a mage "where the ancient mind sleeps" and connected it to the aboleth they had encountered in a lake and to a strange elk they had previously seen watching them from the woods. The precise location of Mordenkainen remains unresolved. At session end, the party debated whether to pursue the Amber Temple, the Fanes, Emil's rescue at Tsolenka Pass, or the Mad Mage. They decided to head toward **Vallaki** for supplies and preparation, particularly for the Amber Temple, while recognizing that several major objectives remain open.
