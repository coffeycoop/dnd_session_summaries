# Session Summary — 2026-07-09

## Concise Recap

The session began at **Van Richten's Tower** with Ezmerelda d'Avenir giving Diesel a personal tarokka reading. She presented two possible paths. The first identified a **Paladin**: someone who controls their own destiny rather than allowing others to direct them. Its associated fate card, **Innocent**, suggested companionship with a pure person, personal growth, and joy. The second identified a **Berserker**: someone who acts only on impulse, is easily controlled, and loses sight of right and wrong. Its fate card, **Broken One**, warned of loneliness, unhappiness, and never attaining one's heart's desire. Ezmerelda stressed that the cards show possible consequences of choices rather than an unavoidable fate. Diesel appeared receptive to the idea of choosing his own path.

Pidlwick chose to continue travelling with the party rather than remain at the tower. He climbed onto Ramesses, Van Richten's saber-toothed tiger, as if attempting to use him as a mount. Frangela also articulated a personal change in direction: she is no longer acting as an artificer and now sees herself as a rogue/cleric who will still steal, but "for the greater good." Before the group departed, Ezmerelda privately warned Frangela and Aniela that van Richten may know the ritual materials for resealing Vampyr but probably does not understand the ritual's full procedure. She urged them to search the Amber Temple itself for reliable instructions. Ezmerelda also agreed that Ah'man's hair is likely suitable for the requirement that the ritual use a lock of hair from a living evil person, provided Ah'man survives until then.

The party travelled to **Vallaki** to prepare for the Amber Temple. They acquired a large supply of salt and arranged for blessed candles, both needed for the Vampyr ritual. Nazareth also bought a yellow Vistani dress and charcoal while the group shopped. Vallaki residents mentioned that Horatio was collecting money for the orphanage and apparently helping its children, though a local card player also described mysteriously losing a bag of gold after playing cards with him. The party did not pursue this lead. They also decided not to check in on Ireena during this trip, prioritizing Amber Temple preparations.

At the Vistani/dusk elf camp outside Vallaki, the party recruited **Kasimir Velikov** for the journey. Kasimir reminded them that he seeks his sister **Patrina**, who he believes is in the Amber Temple. He believes she may still somehow be communicating with him despite having died centuries ago. Kasimir also recited the Mad Mage poem, which refers to "where the ancient mind sleeps in willows bow but never weep" and warns of strange, reality-altering magic in the forest north of the lake. He described Vistani who entered that area and returned enchanted, including one who believed she had been turned into a cat. The party recognized the continuing Mad Mage lead but chose not to divert to investigate it.

Rather than take the direct mountain route immediately, the group travelled south toward **Berez** using their cart as a raft on the river. Diesel, in a salmon-like aquatic form, steered the raft. Near the flooded ruins of Berez, they located a ring of moss-glowing stones: the **Swamp Fane**, shrine of the Weaver. A sick goat covered in sores briefly appeared near the shrine and fled into the swamp. Jobadia attempted to speak a reconsecration in Druidic while in a bizarre two-goat form with loud bee wings, but nothing happened. Aniela then addressed the Weaver directly and offered the heart of **Baba Lysaga**, whom the party believed to be the Weaver's greatest enemy.

The rite activated, separating the people inside the stone circle from those outside and placing those inside in a temporal vision. They saw time race backward through more than a hundred seasons and watched Berez before its destruction. First, they saw Strahd flood the living village by summoning the river in rage; the flood killed its inhabitants. They then saw a woman who closely resembled Ireena, younger and more richly dressed, meet Strahd in the circle. She promised to meet him the following night after saying goodbye to her family. On the next day, they saw her drowned and burned on a funeral pyre. The vision then moved further back to the original desecration of the Swamp Fane: Strahd and Baba Lysaga had bound five teenage human druids to the Fane's stones, tortured them, and removed their hearts. The last survivor mourned that "my ladies are dead" before Baba Lysaga killed her, cut out her heart, and gave it to Strahd to consume. The Fane's magic then disappeared.

After the vision, the heart had vanished from the dagger. **Jenny**, the elderly woman known to the party, briefly appeared in the circle dressed in hides and flowers, holding the heart. She smiled, nodded, and faded with it. The party realized Jenny is the **Weaver**. The Swamp Fane was successfully reconsecrated, and Strahd lost the permanent nondetection protection granted by that desecrated Fane. The heart's removal also left the party's talking dagger agitated because Baba Lysaga's heart had been keeping it calm.

The party continued toward **Tsolenka Pass** and the Amber Temple. Their cart could not traverse the narrow, steep mountain path, so they concealed it with foliage at the base of the pass. The climb became extremely cold, windy, snowy, and dark. Everyone except Diesel and Kasimir took a level of exhaustion from exposure, though party members used magical warming where possible. At the top, they reached a gatehouse guarded by unmoving humanoid and animal-shaped figures and blocked by a permanent curtain of green fire. Kasimir tested it, took fire damage, and determined that *dispel magic* could only suppress the enchantment briefly. He dispelled the flames, giving the group one minute to run through the gate.

Inside the gatehouse, the party found a tower. A scout observed **Anastrasya Karelova**, one of Strahd's consorts, on the second floor. She was pouring a smoking, painful alchemical substance over **Emil**, who was chained to a table and matched Zuleika's description. The room contained an alchemical worktable, a large pile of bones and clothing scraps suggesting earlier victims, a portable coffin, and a ladder to a dark third floor. Rather than finish the encounter, the party immediately began to confront Anastrasya and demand Emil's release. The session ended at that moment, before combat, negotiation, or rescue could be resolved.
