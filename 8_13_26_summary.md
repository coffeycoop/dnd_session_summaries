# Session Summary — 2026-08-13

## Concise Recap

The session opened during the party's infiltration of the **Tsolenka Pass tower** where **Anastrasya Karelova** was holding and experimenting on **Emil**. Overnight magical disturbances had caused several body swaps within the party: Diesel and Ah'man exchanged bodies, Aniela and Jobadia/Reggae Joe exchanged bodies, and Frangela and Nazareth exchanged bodies. Each player retained their own character abilities, but the swapped bodies and gear created practical complications. Nazareth used *disguise self* to appear normal despite the swap. The body-swapping effect also meant that damage to one member of the Aniela/Jobadia pair affected the other, a dangerous complication during combat.

The party found Anastrasya's laboratory on the tower's second floor. Emil was chained to a table and suffering from her alchemical experiments. The room held an alchemical worktable, chemicals and medical instruments, a portable coffin, a large pile of bones and clothing, and several hostile specters. Anastrasya had removed a **moonstone** from Emil; with it gone, Emil reverted from his wolf-like state to human form and became calmer, though he was exhausted and unable to help much. Frangela successfully picked the restraints' locks after another party member had loosened them with force, freeing Emil from the table.

The party fought the specters while Anastrasya attempted to flee upward. The group used magical and silvered weapons, which were effective against the incorporeal undead. Jobadia, in Aniela's body, tried to embrace and reason with a specter; the attempt hurt both Jobadia and Aniela through the active body-swap link. The party nevertheless defeated or dispersed the specters and continued upstairs. Emil, freed and furious, rejected an offer to cooperate immediately; he wanted to return to the werewolf den and reclaim his position. Confused by the body swaps, he challenged the person he believed was Jobadia to a duel at the next full moon for control of the den, then left the tower alone. The party allowed him to go.

On the top floor, Anastrasya's fear effect finally ended. Jobadia briefly restrained her, but she broke free. Nazareth badly wounded her and forced a negotiation. Rather than kill her, the party offered an alliance against Strahd. Anastrasya believed the party could not defeat Strahd because of his many defenses, but became interested after learning that the party had already restored one Fane and understood the others. She admitted resentment of **Ludmilla Vilisevic**, whom she called the dominant bride, and agreed to keep the party's plans secret. If the party demonstrates that it has lowered Strahd's defenses and signals readiness, she will strike when they confront Strahd in Castle Ravenloft. This is a conditional and self-interested alliance, not a trustworthy redemption.

Anastrasya also revealed that the tower's teleportation circle has a permanent endpoint inside **Castle Ravenloft**. A party member tested it, appearing briefly in a dark, previously unexplored Ravenloft room containing another teleportation circle, then returning safely to the tower. This creates a potential infiltration route into the castle. Anastrasya explained that turning a character into a vampire would make them her thrall; for a turned vampire to become independent, they would have to kill the vampire who turned them. The party declined her offer of vampirism. She returned to her portable coffin to sleep and told the party to contact her after they had weakened Strahd.

The party searched Anastrasya's laboratory. Her notebooks showed no larger plan; they were records of trial-and-error experiments on Emil, including sunlight exposure, moonstone placement, silver exposure, physical manipulation, and alchemical substances. Party members took miscellaneous vials, reagents, and medical supplies. They also took a lock of Anastrasya's hair while she slept, providing a stronger candidate for the ritual's "living evil person" hair component than Ah'man's hair. The recovered moonstone emits faint moonlight. A party member began experimenting with attaching it to **Lightbringer** to create a more effective moonlight source, potentially useful for werewolves.

When the party searched the bone pile for warm clothing, it disturbed four skeletons. The skeletons began assembling themselves from the debris, but the party withdrew before they could fully engage and the skeletons did not pursue. The group then left the tower, confirming that Emil had already departed for the werewolf den. Kasimir remained focused on continuing toward the Amber Temple to find his sister Patrina.

The party resumed the climb toward the **Amber Temple**. Extreme cold forced saving throws against exhaustion; those without cold-resistant forms or equipment suffered additional exhaustion. Jobadia used a bear form and Aniela used a snow-leopard form to protect themselves from the cold. At the summit, the group reached the temple facade: a 50-foot-high mountainside entrance decorated with six 20-foot-tall amber statues of faceless, prayerful figures. The party considered breaking off amber from the statues for the Vampyr resealing ritual, but realized that hand-chiseling a cubic foot of amber would take many hours and likely expose them to additional exhaustion.

Nazareth found one set of recent footprints leading into the attached monastery at the temple entrance, proving someone else had recently entered. Inside, the party found a cold, icy hallway with four side doors, a final door leading down, and murals depicting the temple's history. The murals showed wizards quarrying stone, imprisoning evil entities in stone, becoming twisted and monstrous themselves, then killing one another. The final scene showed a lone wizard with hammer and chisel surrounded by evil faces. The open final door bore the warning: "If you seek honor or power, turn back. For these halls hold not but doom. Leave now before our damnation becomes yours." Recent tracks continued down the stone stairs. The session ended with the party at this threshold, preparing to descend into the Amber Temple.
