# Session Summary — 2026-07-23

## Concise Recap

The party, stranded in The Styes with a three-day deadline to return through the summoning portal, pursued an investigation into the Lantern Ghost murders and the corruption of **Councillor Mr. Dory**. After exploring the decrepit **Hopene'er Asylum**, where the wrongly executed fisherman **Jarme Loveage** was allegedly imprisoned before his execution, they discovered evidence of something far darker: a chained, octopus-like creature in the asylum's depths and references to an **aboleth** named **Sgothgah** (the Whisperer), which appeared to be manipulating both the asylum and the city's murder conspiracy.

The party learned that **Officer Jute**, the militia guard who discovered Jarme at the crime scene, was herself murdered and her body left in the streets. They also discovered that **Councillor Dory** runs a warehouse beside a mud pit called **Hemlock Pit**, and that Dory lives in a ship suspended by crane above the pit, filled with hot water (suggesting a condition that requires aquatic immersion). The party determined that four corrupt councillors—Dory, **Thornwell**, **Rashlen**, and **Sliris**—control the militia and likely orchestrate the murders and city corruption.

To prepare for confronting Dory, the party created a trap using **Reggae Joe**'s rumor that "fair maidens" would be frolicking in the northwest quarter at night. This was intended to lure the murderer into a prepared ambush. The plan partially worked: Reggae Joe and **Ah'man Poo'tit'en** encountered a degenerate fisherman in the northeast quarter who attempted to assault **Aniela Grinfeather** (positioned as bait). Reggae Joe charged to save Aniela, both combatants critically failed, and the fisherman escaped into the mud after realizing he'd picked the wrong target.

The party then tracked down and killed two **skum** (aboleth-transformed aquatic servants) in the streets—apparent agents sent to investigate or protect Dory's interests. During the brief combat, the party discovered that **Constable Jute** (the militia guard from the first session) was dead, killed by the skum, and that she bore a militia badge. The encounter with these creatures confirmed that Sgothgah and the aboleth are actively manipulating the city. The party also obtained valuable alchemical supplies (vials of alchemist's fire, poison, oils, and 15 gold) from Dory's warehouse during their investigation.

The session culminated with the party approaching Dory's warehouse after dark, using stealth and supernatural concealment. They cast *pass without trace* to avoid detection and began infiltrating the warehouse, ready to confront Dory directly. The party also created two **ghouls** from the corpses of the slain skum, intending to use them as diversions. The three-day deadline looms, adding urgency to their mission.
