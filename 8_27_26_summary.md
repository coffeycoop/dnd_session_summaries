# Session Summary — 2026-08-27

## Concise Recap

The party resumed in the **Amber Temple** with three flameskulls confronting Reggae Joe at the entrance to the next hall. Nazareth opened the fight with two arrows into one skull, and Frangela's *cloud of daggers*, Aniela's *ice knife*, Kasimir's bow, and the others' attacks finished the encounter. The flameskulls used *magic missile* and repeated green fireballs; Aniela, Reggae Joe, and Diesel took fire damage. Reggae Joe transformed into a giant octopus, using an ink cloud and tentacles while the party destroyed the last two flameskulls.

The party entered a lecture hall with amber reliefs of wizards practicing cantrips. The amber was only a thin veneer and did not provide a large source for the party's planned ritual. Beneath a table they found **Vilnius**, a diseased Barovian commoner who had entered the temple seeking a cure. His restorative treatment repeatedly failed because the disease returned. Vilnius wore a golden medallion that compelled him to keep it and carried the voice of a woman promising freedom if it were returned to her on the bottom floor. Diesel took the medallion and became infected and magically compelled; Reggae Joe restrained him and removed it, after which Aniela used a *remove curse* scroll to deactivate the medallion and end the effects.

Vilnius warned that he had heard large footsteps from the north and wanted to leave. The party allowed him to depart after giving him food and asked him to scrape amber from the walls, though he did so only half-heartedly. Reggae Joe then encountered an enormous amber construct with a raven-shaped head. The construct attacked after Reggae Joe entered its room, but the party broke off its limbs and head with magical weapons and spells. In the central chamber, Reggae Joe climbed onto a 40-foot statue and encountered magical darkness and a hostile voice. After taking heavy necrotic damage from a *finger of death*, he returned to human form. The voice was eventually revealed as **Nephron**, a foxlike spellcasting fiend and the Amber Temple's self-described last remaining guardian. Nephron accepted the party after Reggae Joe shared the secret of Gutter Juice and invited several characters into his hidden home for tea.
