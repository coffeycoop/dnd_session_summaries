# Session Summary — 2026-05-14

## Concise Recap

The party completed their difficult entry into **Van Richten's Tower** on Lake Baratok and reached its furnished fourth floor, where **Dr. Rudolph van Richten** and **Ezmerelda d'Avenir** were waiting. Van Richten, no longer in his **Rictavio** disguise, made it clear that he and Ezmerelda knew the party had been struggling with the tower's entrance and had intentionally allowed them to prove they could get inside. The tower's magic requires the owner's express permission to enter, even through an open door or window. Van Richten's saber-toothed tiger, **Ramesses**, was present and initially ignored the party.

The party used *speak with animals* to communicate with Ramesses. After being fed stored flesh, the tiger identified himself and said that he follows van Richten to eat the spoils of his hunts. He believes the party shares his master's purpose of destroying **Strahd von Zarovich**, but bluntly predicts that they have no chance against Strahd and may eventually become his food. The exchange established Ramesses as intelligent, hungry, and loyal to van Richten rather than friendly to the party.

The party tried to demonstrate that they are capable allies against Strahd. They showed van Richten the **Holy Symbol of Ravenkind**, which they had recovered from Castle Ravenloft and to which a party member is attuned; this genuinely impressed him. They also referenced possessing the **Tome of Strahd** and knowing that the **Amber Temple** is tied to Strahd's power. Van Richten was dismissive of several lesser weapons and of the party's attempts to impress him, but he acknowledged that the holy symbol, the tome, and knowledge of the Amber Temple gave their proposal more credibility.

Van Richten confirmed that he is a legendary vampire hunter and described a previous attempt to defeat Strahd. The archmage **Mordenkainen** brought a Barovian rabble army to Castle Ravenloft despite van Richten's warning that they were unprepared. Van Richten saw Mordenkainen fight Strahd, then fall from the castle into the river below; he believes the archmage was too powerful to have died and suspects that the strange presence or "elk spirit" reported in the forest around Lake Zarovich may be the missing Mad Mage. This explains why van Richten knows Mordenkainen and why he considers a direct assault on Strahd hopeless without preparation.

Van Richten also gave the party a grim explanation of Barovia's cosmology. He said Barovia is an echo of another world that has been taken into the **Domains of Dread**. Souls cannot leave: the dead are generally recycled into another life, while souls consumed by vampires are destroyed. According to him, the only ways home are to pledge themselves to Strahd and become his servants—an option he threatened to punish—or to kill Strahd. He views vampires as irredeemably evil, while his view of werewolves is more pragmatic: he does not call them good, but says they could previously be tolerated when they lived peacefully.

When pressed for actionable intelligence, van Richten began a quid-pro-quo arrangement. He said the Amber Temple was built by ancient mages as a prison for great evils before Strahd and the Barovians arrived. One amber prison has cracked, allowing an imprisoned entity called **Vampyr** to leak power into the world; van Richten believes Vampyr made Strahd what he is and grants him power beyond that of an ordinary vampire lord. He did not provide all he knows, instead offering further information in exchange for two jobs.

**First task:** investigate the werewolf den in the hills between the tower and Krezk. Van Richten says the werewolves once coexisted with nearby people, who left goats and chickens for them at the full moon, but have recently begun killing travelers and increasing their ranks. He suspects they are now under Strahd's sway, even though werewolves and vampires traditionally hate one another. The party may either identify the reason for the change and report back, or eliminate the entire den. In return, van Richten will explain more about Vampyr, the Amber Temple, and the extra power Strahd draws from it.

**Second task:** obtain a book about **the Fanes** from the library at the **Abbey of Saint Markovia** in Krezk. Van Richten does not care whether the party persuades the Abbot or steals it. He admitted that he is unwelcome at the Abbey because he accused the Abbot of being a sadistic, cruel, corrupt, and unwilling servant of Strahd, and may have slapped him. If the party brings him the book, he promises to reveal what he knows about the Fanes and their connection to Strahd's second extraordinary source of power. The party agreed to get the book, though some members openly questioned why van Richten needed it rather than allowing them to read it themselves.

Tension remained high. Diesel grappled and threatened van Richten after judging him a bully for leaving the sentient bust Buster face-down in the tower's library. Ezmerelda intervened and Ramesses became defensive of his master. Van Richten stayed calm and provided the initial Amber Temple information, but warned that he would give no more if the party did not fulfill his requests. The party did not attack him, but their trust in him remained uncertain.

Ezmerelda offered the group shelter and a tarokka reading. The party rested in the tower. Diesel's reading produced **Anarchist**, **Darklord**, and **Executioner**. Ezmerelda interpreted this as a warning that Diesel is independent and difficult to rely upon, is influenced by someone she assumes is Strahd, and may become or encounter an executioner if that influence continues. She emphasized that her readings are less authoritative than Madam Eva's and are meant to illuminate personal choices rather than dictate fate.

During the night, four werewolves prowled the tower's island. One party member climbed to the roof and shot a werewolf with a shortbow, adding Sneak Attack damage; the wolves scattered rather than attacking. Van Richten said the party's earlier noisy efforts at the tower door, including lightning, had drawn the wolves there. The party nevertheless completed a long rest.

The party continued exploring the tower and learned more about its prior owner, **Khazan**, an astronomer-mage. Buster, the sentient bust in the second-floor library, had been knocked from his stand by damage to the tower and was restored by Ezmerelda. Buster dislikes van Richten because van Richten ignored him after arriving, but says van Richten did question him about Strahd, vampires, werewolves, the Fanes, the Wild Folk, and the creature in the lake. Buster can only vouch that van Richten hates what he sees as evil; he cannot say whether van Richten is trustworthy beyond that. Buster also reported that Ezmerelda feels loyal to van Richten because he helped her catch her brother's murderers, though they later fell out.

The party activated a clumsy animated suit of armor by speaking Khazan's name. After its helmet had been removed as a joke, the armor wandered blindly and exposed a hidden alcove behind it. Inside was a velvet-covered box containing an embossed telescoping telescope/spyglass and a sextant. Neither appeared overtly magical. Khazan's damaged astronomical journals state that he came to Barovia to observe a different sky, then recorded that "the mists have come and the stars are gone." The party confirmed that Barovia's sky shows only the diffuse glow of sun and moon through clouds and Mists, with no visible stars. This establishes that Khazan's tower and research predate van Richten's use of it and are not connected to Mordenkainen's failed assault.

In the morning, the party decided to travel to Krezk for the Abbey book. They recognized additional unresolved business in Krezk: a Vistani with powers similar to Madam Eva's is said to be staying there, and they had previously heard of missing orphans, including one reportedly interested in visiting the lake near the tower. The group traveled roughly two hours from the tower to Krezk, passing harvested fields and reaching the village's stone walls. **Ah'man Poo'tit'en** slipped past the gate guard, Oleg, while the rest of the party dealt with entry formalities. The guard went to fetch Krezk's burgomaster. The available D&D transcript ends as the burgomaster arrives to receive the party.

## Entities

- **Rudolph van Richten / Rictavio:** Monster hunter; seeks Strahd's destruction; offers knowledge in exchange for tasks; considers vampires irredeemably evil; distrusts the Abbot.
- **Ezmerelda d'Avenir:** Monster hunter and van Richten's former student/associate; housed the party and conducted Diesel's reading; remains loyal to van Richten despite a past falling-out.
- **Ramesses:** Van Richten's intelligent saber-toothed tiger; speaks through *speak with animals*; wants meat and is loyal to his master.
- **Buster:** Sentient bust in Khazan's library; loves Ezmerelda, dislikes van Richten for ignoring him, and supplies partial information about both.
- **Khazan:** Long-absent astronomer-mage and original owner of the tower; his journals document the disappearance of Barovia's stars after the Mists arrived.
- **Mordenkainen / the Mad Mage:** Archmage who led an unsuccessful attack on Castle Ravenloft and fell into the river; van Richten suspects he survived and may be near Lake Zarovich.
- **Vampyr:** Entity allegedly imprisoned in the Amber Temple; van Richten believes it transformed Strahd and continues to empower him. This is van Richten's claim, not independently verified by the party.
- **The Abbot:** Ruler of the Abbey of Saint Markovia; van Richten alleges the Abbot is corrupt and unwillingly allied with Strahd, based on his instincts rather than evidence presented to the party.
- **Oleg:** Young Krezk gate guard who stopped the party and summoned the burgomaster.

## Locations

- **Van Richten's Tower / Khazan's Tower:** A magical, hard-to-enter tower on Lake Baratok; now used by van Richten and Ezmerelda. Its library holds Khazan's astronomical works and Buster.
- **Lake Baratok area:** Location of the tower and nearby werewolf activity; van Richten links the local forest to possible signs of Mordenkainen.
- **Amber Temple:** Ancient prison for evil entities; alleged origin of Vampyr's power over Strahd.
- **Werewolf Den:** In the hills between the tower and Krezk; target of van Richten's first task.
- **Krezk:** Walled western Barovian village reached at session end; the Abbey is on its high hill.
- **Abbey of Saint Markovia:** Holds the requested book about the Fanes; controlled by the Abbot, who is hostile to van Richten.
- **Castle Ravenloft:** Site of the party's recovery of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind and of Mordenkainen's failed battle with Strahd.

## Revelations

- Van Richten says Barovia is part of the Domains of Dread and souls cannot leave it normally.
- Van Richten says killing Strahd is the only acceptable route home; serving Strahd might permit travel but would make the party his minions.
- Van Richten believes Strahd has at least two extraordinary power sources: Vampyr/Amber Temple and the Fanes.
- The Amber Temple predates Strahd and was made as a prison for great evils; one amber prison is said to be cracked.
- Khazan's records show that Barovia once had visible stars and that the Mists erased them from the sky.
- The werewolf pack's recent violence is a change from its former, relatively tolerated behavior and may be connected to Strahd's influence.
