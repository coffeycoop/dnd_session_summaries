# Session Summary — 2026-09-17

## Concise Recap

The party resumed its exploration of the **Amber Temple** at the secret royal room discovered at the end of the previous session. Inside, they met a confused lich who identified himself, after prompting, as **Exethanter**. His memory was impaired by a **mind blank** spell, and the party temporarily suppressed that effect with *Dispel Magic*. They learned that he was once one of the temple's wizards, that the resident wizards were gradually driven mad by the concentration of evil in the temple, and that he became a champion of the Dark Power **Tenebrous** as a way to preserve his mind. He described the Dark Powers as competing through champions and warned that one of them may have escaped its prison.

Exethanter revealed that the apparently hollow section of wall was the entrance to his library. He explained that the books are preserved only inside the library and decay if removed. He also provided the code words for the temple's arcane-locked doors, described the stairways leading toward the amber vaults, and explained a possible ritual for imprisoning a Dark Power in a new amber prison. The party used the library's resources to prepare holy water and a powerful healing potion, restored Diesel's senses and mobility, and took a long rest. Frangela learned the *Spare the Dying* cantrip, while Diesel developed a new long-term madness fixation on a lucky charm.

The party then descended into an amber vault containing cracked amber formations and several crude wooden coffins. They suspected that the coffins concealed vampires and opened them cautiously. Several vampire spawn were killed before they could raise an alarm, using attacks to the heart, including a wooden arrow and Reggae Joe's magically extended living staff. The vault's actual imprisoned Dark Power was not identified, and the session ended when Kasimir stood before an amber sarcophagus and pleaded for Patrina to be returned, apparently preparing to bargain with or release whatever was inside.

## Events

- The party continued its conversation with the lich in the hidden royal chamber and maintained the cleaning-service deception long enough to question him.
- The lich repeatedly described a missing spellbook and could not initially remember his name, his location, or the temple's layout.
- Frangela proposed names including **Mordenkainen**; the lich rejected that name. He eventually accepted **Exethanter** as a name he had once used. The transcript's pronunciation varied, but the entity was clearly the same lich.
- Nazareth cast *Dispel Magic* on Exethanter. The spell temporarily reduced the effect of *Mind Blank*, allowing Exethanter to remember more about the Amber Temple, its library, and the Dark Powers.
- Exethanter said the temple's wizard order had deteriorated into madness and violence. He survived by offering himself as a champion of Tenebrous, a Dark Power associated with lichdom and destructive use of life force.
- Exethanter claimed that Tenebrous remained contained in the basement and that he had found a loophole allowing him to resist the command to destroy everyone around him.
- Exethanter recognized General von Zarovich, apparently **Strahd von Zarovich**, as a former visitor who studied the temple's lore and asked questions about rituals and the Dark Powers. He showed the party gifts that he said Strahd had brought from his childhood home.
- Exethanter identified the Sunsword as a weapon previously carried by Sergei von Zarovich and said that recharging it would require a powerful wizard with the appropriate ability.
- Exethanter identified the skull alcove as a place where he stored the heads of wizard companions whom he had killed after they became monstrous. He said some bodies continued to regrow after decapitation.
- Exethanter disclosed the concealed entrance to his library by demonstrating the same shaving-and-haircut motion used to open the other secret stone.
- The party entered the library and confirmed that its books were protected from decay only while inside the room. Exethanter warned that books removed from the library would decay within moments.
- Exethanter showed the party a recipe for an **arcane greater healing potion**. The recipe required holy water and unusual ingredients; the party could not simply remove the recipe's knowledge from the library, but Reggae Joe was allowed to write it down.
- Reggae Joe used water from the decanter of endless water and a blessing from Frangela to make holy water, then combined it with the dried potion ingredients. The resulting potion cured Diesel's current madness effect and restored his hearing and other impaired senses, while also removing the need for the temporary peg leg.
- The party discussed the difference between *Divination* and *Channel Divinity*. Exethanter warned that the Dark Powers could intercept divination or psychic communication, potentially revealing the party's location to Strahd or another powerful entity.
- Exethanter demonstrated the influence of a Dark Power by casting a green-flamed sphere. He said the unusual color was evidence that one of the Dark Powers might be loose, but he did not identify which one.
- Kasimir said he could hear Patrina Velikovna calling from below. The party questioned whether this was really Patrina, a magical lure, or another consequence of the temple's madness.
- Exethanter gave the party the code word **Davaton** for one of the locked doors and drew a map showing the code words for the other arcane-locked doors.
- The library's hidden wall opened onto two stairways. One led toward an amber vault containing three amber sarcophagi; the other led toward the back of the central statue and served as a security route.
- Exethanter explained a ritual for imprisoning a Dark Power in a new cubic-foot amber prison. The ritual would require a suitable sarcophagus or vault location, a salt ring, the hair of an evil person, six candles, and the name of the entity. The party would have to maintain the ritual for several rounds while surviving the summoned fiend's attack.
- Exethanter said that an already imprisoned entity would not emerge merely because it was named, but an unconfined one would appear and try to kill the participants. He advised looking for a cracked or compromised sarcophagus if the party intended to attempt the ritual.
- The party discussed the Dark Power associated with vampirism and the possibility that Strahd's immortality could only be removed by dealing with the Dark Power that empowered him. Exethanter said killing a champion without imprisoning the relevant Dark Power would not permanently solve the problem.
- Exethanter described vampire weaknesses and regeneration, including the importance of a wooden stake through the heart while a vampire is in its coffin. The party decided to test that method on the vault's occupants.
- Exethanter offered several possible magical preparations from his spellbook, including *Haste*, *Greater Invisibility*, *Stoneskin*, and *Enlarge/Reduce*. The party chose to retain a free casting of *Haste* rather than use it immediately.
- The party took a long rest in the library. Exethanter disappeared through the secret passage afterward, leaving the party unable to access his books normally; the books appeared blank without his presence.
- Frangela learned **Spare the Dying** as a new cleric cantrip.
- The party made additional madness checks after sleeping in the Amber Temple. Frangela briefly woke screaming and fainted, while Diesel developed a long-term fixation on a lucky charm that he believed was essential to his strength and functioning.
- Reggae Joe rushed down the stairs to search for traps and transformed into a mole rat to scout a tunnel. The tunnel led to a sealed treasury containing piles of treasure and a motionless amber golem, rather than directly to the expected threat.
- The lower vault contained a cold, amber-covered chamber with sculpted walls, three tall rough amber blocks, two cracked areas spilling rubble, and six crude wooden coffin-like boxes.
- Nazareth cast *Dispel Magic* on the amber formations, but nothing visibly changed. The party did not confirm that the formations were illusions or identify the Dark Power imprisoned there.
- Reggae Joe pushed over one of the wooden boxes. A female vampire spawn emerged, but Nazareth killed her before she could call for help.
- The party quietly opened the remaining boxes. Additional vampire spawn were killed while still sleeping, including one killed with a wooden arrow and another pierced through the heart by Reggae Joe's magically extended staff.
- The final awakened vampire spawn attacked Diesel and briefly seized his weapon. Frangela struck her from hiding, Reggae Joe pierced her with the extended staff, and a fourth-level *Call Lightning* finished her.
- Kasimir ignored the party's request to check the other amber formations and instead stood before one of them, touched it, and pleaded, “Bring her back. I'll do whatever you want.”

## Revelations

- Exethanter's *Mind Blank* was suppressing his memories rather than erasing his identity permanently. *Dispel Magic* weakened the effect for approximately an hour.
- The Amber Temple's wizard order was destroyed by madness, violence, and transformation caused by prolonged exposure to the Dark Powers.
- Exethanter became a champion of Tenebrous as a survival measure. He described championhood as a curse rather than a gift.
- Dark Powers can draw strength through champions and may be able to intercept divination or psychic communication.
- Exethanter's account further connects Strahd von Zarovich to the Amber Temple's lore, rituals, and Dark Powers, but it does not prove which Dark Power empowered him.
- The hidden hollow wall was the library entrance. The library is not merely a room of books: its magic preserves the contents only while they remain inside.
- The Amber Temple's arcane-locked doors have code words. **Davaton** was explicitly spoken for one door, and Exethanter provided a map of additional codes.
- The party learned a possible method for trapping a Dark Power in a new amber prison, but the ritual has only one apparent attempt because several components are consumed.
- A vampire's coffin provides a major vulnerability: the party successfully killed sleeping vampire spawn by piercing their hearts with wood.
- The lower vault's crude coffins contained vampire spawn, but the relationship between those creatures and the large amber prisons remains unresolved.
- The Icon of Ravenloft can provide protection against fiends and undead within thirty feet, including protection from being charmed, frightened, or possessed by them. The party discussed but did not clearly activate every available property.
