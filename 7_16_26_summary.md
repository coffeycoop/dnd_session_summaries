# Session Summary — 2026-07-16

## Concise Recap

The party was unexpectedly summoned from Barovia through a misused summoning scroll created by **Perin**, a human warlock. Perin, attempting to use cheaper ingredients than recommended for an expensive summoning ritual, inadvertently pulled the entire party away from the teleportation circle they had just used in the Amber Temple's gatehouse—where they had discovered a vampire lady and Emil's whereabouts. The party materialized in The Styes, a decaying industrial port district filled with pollution, poverty, and despair. They emerged in a collapsed, mildewed warehouse near the polluted docks, disoriented and suffering sensory damage from the acidic smog, sudden bright light, and chemical burn.

Perin explained that he had left them two weeks prior, was searching for **Bruce** (a missing party member), and found a merchant who sold him a summoning scroll promising great ingredients but warning of potentially fatal consequences if they did not return through the portal within three days. The ritual worked, though imperfectly, and now the party has a ticking deadline to find a way home.

The group explored the immediate area and discovered they were in a slum called the Alchemists' Quarter, surrounded by ramshackle buildings, rancid water, floating garbage, and desperate people. They encountered **Master Refrum**, a priest and philanthropist being chased by a mob of street children who blamed him for recent murders in the city. The party protected Refrum, won over the children with Reggae Joe's performance and good berries, and learned that the city has been plagued by murders attributed to a "**Lantern Ghost**"—a killer or killers the populace blames for multiple deaths.

Refrum revealed that his friend, a fisherman named **Jarme Loveage**, was wrongly executed for the murders. Despite his execution, the killings continued. Refrum suspects a councillor named **Mr. Dory** was involved and is connected to the murders. The party was also wary of Refrum after the children's accusations, but eventually agreed to help investigate and escort him to his workshop. On the way, a small militia force stopped them, demanding a "newcomer tax." Perin, in disguise as a grubby child, charmed the guards using *charm person* and convinced them to let the party pass without payment. The guards warned them to avoid trouble and stay "clean in the Styes"—suggesting that being conspicuous or appearing out of place draws unwanted attention from authorities.

The party learned that the city's guards and councillors appear corrupt, demanding tribute from newcomers and having a reputation for violence. They established a temporary shelter at Refrum's workshop, though Refrum's loyalties and full involvement in city politics remain unclear. The session ended with the party beginning their investigation of the murders and the possibility that a councillor is the true culprit.
