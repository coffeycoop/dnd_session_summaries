# Session Summary — 2026-06-18

## Concise Recap

The session resumed during the party's divided confrontation at the **Abbey of Saint Markovia**. On the ground floor, Diesel, Ah'man, and Aniela finished pursuing the last visible mongrelfolk residents. Diesel used his heavy crossbow to wound, push, and knock down a fleeing mongrelfolk; Ah'man then shot the badly injured creature after moving into range. The party treated the Abbey's remaining mongrelfolk as enemies despite their confinement and fear, and the asylum wing was effectively cleared of the residents encountered by the party.

Upstairs, the party continued fighting the Abbot. **Jobadia Baskin / Reggae Joe** was still affected by long-term madness from the prior session and believed he was the king of Barovia's werewolves. He was also undergoing an involuntary screaming fit from the Abbot's psychic attacks. The Abbot's attacks overwhelmed Jobadia: damage from poison, radiant effects, and *spirit guardians* dropped him unconscious, causing his summoned fire elemental to disappear. Another party member stabilized him and used a 3rd-level *cure wounds* to restore 25 hit points. This ended Jobadia's current screaming fit, but not his six-day belief that he is a werewolf leader.

The Abbot did not die in the confrontation. He was pulled through a portal under hostile or involuntary circumstances; as the portal closed, a severed hand holding an old, thick iron skeleton key was left behind. The party took the key and used it to access the Abbot's office. The exact sequence that caused the Abbot's departure is partly unavailable because the transcript contains declared gaps, but the outcome is clear: the Abbot escaped, and the party gained access to his private library.

The Abbot's office was neglected except for the desk and bookshelves, which were maintained and actively used. The party found the long, leather-bound **book about the Fanes** that van Richten had requested. Other books were largely medical, herbal, and religious works. This completed the immediate retrieval objective, though the party did not yet know the book's full contents.

Meanwhile, **Frangela Brethil** escorted Vasilka from the Abbey down to Krezk. Frangela showed Vasilka ordinary parts of the outside world and gently prepared her for the fact that townspeople might react to her unusual appearance. Vasilka repeatedly asked whether her repaired face was acceptable and what people in town were like. At Krezk, the burgomaster provided the party a small stable room for the night and accepted that Vasilka could stay in the town rather than remain at the Abbey. Frangela explicitly told Vasilka that she could choose her own future rather than become Strahd's bride. Vasilka did not yet understand love or independent choice well; after discussion, she chose to stay in Krezk, attend school, and eventually imagined marrying the town teacher. The party also discussed bringing her to Dr. van Richten for medical help. Vasilka's needs remain unusual: she stands still in a corner while "sleeping" and periodically consumes a soft, squishy substance to avoid drying out. She accompanied the party to Van Richten's Tower the following morning, so her longer-term placement remains unsettled.

After the party took a long rest in Krezk, Nazareth read part of the Fanes book. It described the Fanes as three ancient nature spirits and their associated stone-ring shrines, predating the Barovians and worship of the Morninglord. The **Weaver** is associated with the **Swamp Fane**, the **Huntress** with the **Mountain Fane**, and the **Seeker** with the **Forest Fane**. The old Wild Folk served these spirits and were granted the ability to take animal forms, including wolves and ravens. In that earlier era, wolves were part of the natural balance rather than corrupted servants of Strahd.

Van Richten's later explanation completed the core revelation. Strahd desecrated all three Fanes and stole power from their spirits. The desecrations grant him substantial protections, including protection from divination/scrying, resistance to fire, cold, and lightning, stronger physical defenses, and influence over natural forces such as water, wind, and wildlife. The exact mechanics and the full rites to reconsecrate every Fane are not completely preserved in the available transcript, but the party learned that reconsecration requires more than visiting a stone circle: it requires a meaningful service to the relevant Fane. For the Weaver's Swamp Fane, the book describes killing her greatest enemy and placing that enemy's heart as an offering. The party believed it might already possess a relevant heart, but did not confirm that it was the correct one.

The party then returned to **Van Richten's Tower** with the book and Vasilka. They entered through the tower's established magical sequence, collected Buster from the library, and met Rudolph van Richten, Ezmerelda d'Avenir, and Ramesses again on the top floor. Van Richten examined the book and explained the Fanes' history and the benefits Strahd receives from their desecration. He also confirmed that Krezk's blessed pool cannot cure Jobadia's present madness a second time; the condition requires *greater restoration* or another capable priest, likely with diamond material components.

The group considered several possible next steps: locating and reconsecrating a Fane, continuing the Fanes investigation, or pursuing the werewolf den. They chose the werewolf lead because Krezk's burgomaster's son Ilya and another boy remain captive, and the next full moon is approaching. The party also clarified that werewolves are humanoids with lycanthropy, not beasts: animal-control magic such as *animal friendship* will not work on them. The session ended with the party committed to pursue the werewolf situation next, before the full moon.
