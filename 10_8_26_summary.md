# Session Summary — 2026-10-08

## Concise Recap

The party continued its exploration of the **Amber Temple** after Kasimir Velikov touched an amber sarcophagus and communed with a dark, avian-looking entity. Kasimir said the entity—identified in the transcript as **Zudan**, “the Corpse Star”—had promised to resurrect his sister **Patrina Velikovna** and give the party gifts if they imprisoned **Vampir**, a rival Dark Power that had escaped its prison. Kasimir received a resurrection vial and a greater-invisibility vial for his planned infiltration of **Castle Ravenloft**, then left to retrieve Patrina. The party received individually selected smaller vials; Reggae Joe drank his and gained the Lucky feat, while the others generally retained or considered their gifts.

The party searched a connected treasure room, taking a 500-gp diamond and a **glamoured studded leather** armor, then retreated when an amber golem began throwing rocks. They returned to the library and briefly maintained their cleaning-service cover with **Exethanter**, then explored additional chambers. Frangela used Exethanter’s door code, **Davaton**, to open another amber vault containing two intact sarcophagi and one shattered sarcophagus. The group decided to attempt the Dark Power imprisonment ritual using the shattered vault area, salt, blessed candles, evil hair, and a cubic foot of amber.

Diesel entered the salt circle holding the hair and called **Vampir** into the ritual. A towering, primeval, vampire-like aberration appeared outside the circle while a dark essence began forming in the amber. The manifestation caused severe madness effects: Frangela collapsed into a stupor, Aniela panicked and fled, and Reggae Joe suffered additional amnesia. Reggae Joe cast a guardian-spirit spell and tried to rouse Frangela; Aniela cast *Flaming Sphere* while fleeing. Vampir then bit and grappled Frangela, poisoning her and preventing her from regaining hit points. The session ended after the first round, with the ritual incomplete and Vampir still present.

## Events

- Kasimir touched an amber sarcophagus and entered a trance. A dark, vaguely humanoid avian entity appeared within it and spoke telepathically.
- Frangela’s Religion knowledge identified the being as **Zudan**, also called the **Corpse Star**, a dangerous entity associated in the transcript with control over life and death and the ability to restore the dead. This identification and lore came from the game’s campaign narration; the root Curse of Strahd corpus does not independently establish Zudan.
- Reggae Joe touched Kasimir and was drawn into the same otherworldly field. Zudan stated that a deal had been struck with Kasimir and allowed Reggae Joe to leave without offering him a separate bargain.

- Kasimir displayed a warm, golden vial that he believed would fully resurrect Patrina and a dark vial granting a form of greater invisibility. He said Zudan had told him where Patrina could be found in the crypts of Castle Ravenloft.

The group briefly resumed its cleaning-service deception with Exethanter, who continued muttering about his missing spellbook and called after them to replace the skulls in the closet.

- Diesel invoked Vampir in a makeshift version of the ritual. Wind rose, the candles fluttered, the room became hot, loud breathing was heard, and a dark shape began forming inside the amber.
- Vampir manifested outside the salt circle as a ten-foot-tall gray, stone-skinned, excessively proportioned creature resembling a primeval vampire. The narration identified him as an **aberration** and as something more like the source or god of vampires than an ordinary vampire lord such as Strahd.

Reggae Joe gained a second long-term amnesia effect lasting three days. Diesel resisted the immediate madness effect, while Nazareth was too affected or distracted to intervene normally.
- Reggae Joe cast a fourth-rank guardian-spirit spell, described in the transcript as a healing/supporting effect, and attempted to rouse Frangela by slapping her and calling for her to wake up.
- Aniela cast *Flaming Sphere* at Vampir while fleeing. Vampir succeeded on the Dexterity save and took 8 fire damage; the transcript does not establish that this was a meaningful injury.
