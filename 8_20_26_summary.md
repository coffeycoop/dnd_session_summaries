# Session Summary — 2026-08-20

## Concise Recap

The party continued exploring the **Amber Temple** after reaching the proving rooms. In one room they found three young wild folk—**Graza**, **Radovan**, and **Jesper**—sitting around a makeshift fire in a distressed, trance-like state. A fourth member of their group, **Laszlo**, was already dead and badly charred. The young men initially believed the party was another illusion created by the temple. The party exploited that belief with a silent image of **Strahd**, while Reggae Joe entered in polar-bear form and searched the room. The deception unsettled the young people but did not fully break the effect.

The party eventually made contact by invoking **Kevan**, whose poisoned dagger was recognized by the wild folk. They explained that they had known Kevan, killed the hag responsible for his death, and reconsecrated the swamp Fane. Graza and Radovan accepted that the party was real and followed their instructions to move away from the danger. Jesper remained psychologically affected and continued clutching her head. The party chose not to recruit the three into its expedition: they insisted on remaining to complete their tribe's coming-of-age trial, but agreed to guard the entrance and provide a possible retreat point.

Opening a nearby door revealed the **Upper West Hall**, a scorched amber-lined corridor containing a charred humanoid corpse, arrow slits, and three **flameskulls**. The flameskulls attacked with green fireballs and an upcast *magic missile*. Nazareth and Reggae Joe took heavy damage, and Diesel was burned while standing in the fire in a deep state of depression from being stuck in Ah'Man's grotesque body. Reggae Joe conjured ethereal vampire bats, while Nazareth and Frangela attacked directly and Kasimir fired an arrow. The party destroyed all three flameskulls and healed afterward. The corpse was too badly burned to identify. Frangi saved Diesel from his self-immolation in the fire.

The party then took a long rest in the proving room, which the wild folk described as the safest place in the temple. The body-swapping effect from the previous session ended during the night, returning everyone to their normal bodies. Diesel rejoiced. However, the temple's persistent psychic influence caused confusing whispers and borrowed emotions. Frangela and Diesel failed their madness checks and suffered short-term effects for ten minutes after waking; Frangela had a screaming fit, while Diesel became convinced that the Amber Temple was his palace. Reggae Joe's long-term madness was reduced by one day. During the rest, Kasimir searched for secrets and discovered a hidden repository of ancient scrolls, but the parchment crumbled because of centuries of decay.

After resting, the party searched additional rooms rather than immediately following the main corridor. They found abandoned barracks and two concealed guard rooms. One held the skeleton of a wizard in blue robes, an unusable ancient wand, a knife, and **dimensional shackles**. Crude writing on the wall included the words “voices,” “eyes in the dark,” and “evil,” suggesting that the wizard went mad before dying. The party did not establish that this skeleton was **Mordenkainen**. Tracks several days old continued through an eastern doorway. The session ended after the party opened another hall and three apparently dormant skulls burst into green flame; one was already preparing a spell. Combat was left unresolved.
