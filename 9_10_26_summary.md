# Session Summary — 2026-09-10

## Concise Recap

The party continued exploring the **Amber Temple** after splitting up around Nephron's hidden dwelling. Nephron hosted Reggae Joe, Diesel, and Nazareth for tea while Aniela, Frangela, and Kasimir investigated a ruined treasury. Nephron explained that the temple is a prison for dark powers and claimed that General von Zarovich had once come to the temple, taken secrets from its librarian, and become a champion of one of the Dark Powers before becoming a vampire. He also repeatedly pressed the party for arcane knowledge. The tea ended when Reggae Joe tried to leave and Nephron vanished rather than accompany them.

In the treasury, the party met **Luzon**, a partially corporeal wizard who endlessly tried to restore the plundered room and insisted that the amber golem belonged there. They found crushed remains, old thieves' equipment, and an enchanted **Rhythm-Maker's Drum** intended for a bard. Luzon warned them about the temple's secret passages and arcane locks. The party's attempts to force one locked door caused serious necrotic damage, and prolonged exposure to the temple triggered madness: Frangela hallucinated snakes, Aniela became temporarily nihilistic, and Diesel believed he had a disabling physical ailment. Reggae Joe improvised a peg leg for Diesel, and Kasimir's magic helped move him through the temple.

The party searched additional shrines, guard posts, a banquet hall, and a series of hidden rooms. The banquet feast was an illusion populated by ghostly images of young wizard initiates; a real **decanter of endless water** was concealed among the false food. They also recovered architectural plans and a **Tome of Understanding**, which can permanently increase one reader's Wisdom by two but will not become readable again for a century after use. Reggae Joe used the decanter and old potion remnants to produce a large quantity of Gutter Juice. By striking a hollow wall with a skeleton's arm, Aniela located a secret alcove containing hundreds of old human skulls. The party eventually opened a second secret door and ended the session face-to-face with a lich in a dust-covered chamber decorated like a royal room.
